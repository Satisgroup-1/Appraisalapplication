## 2a. Five discovery counts the pages are waiting on

Each project page now describes what the engagement is **measuring** and
publishes no figure from it. That is deliberate: the numbers do not exist in
anything supplied to us, and a plausible-looking count invites more reliance
than a vague statement does.

Every page carries the method. None carries a chart. Supply an extract against
one of the specifications below and its chart comes back, with a note saying
the figure was counted, over what sample and in what window.

| Project | The one thing being counted | What you supply |
|---|---|---|
| `yacht-operations` | Distinct places a colleague opens before a current answer to a customer status question exists. One integer per project. | A spread of live projects covering short service, mid-length, refit, one that changed hands internally, one returning customer. Counted twice: before release and after a full working month of use. ☐ |
| `cold-chain` | Every alert in one month at one chilled site, sorted into complete, accounted for, recoverable, or dark. | The alert extract plus the maintenance log, shift book, QA dispositions and the named shift managers for those nights. One pass, one classifier, one-week cut-off on asking colleagues. ☐ |
| `property-pipeline` | Transactions whose recorded stage is not supported by the evidence that stage implies, by five kinds of gap. | One asset class, live and recently completed files, taken whole or as a consecutive run rather than a selection. Read by someone who does not handle the files. ☐ |
| `professional-services-intake` | Referrals arriving complete on each of five required items, at first pass, before any clarifying question. | Every referral to one named service line over a continuous window. Definitions frozen before marking starts; a subset double-marked to check agreement. No client or counterparty names leave the firm. ☐ |
| `field-service-planning` | How heavily hard conditions bind the plan, and how often published commitments move. | One dispatch region, six consecutive weeks with no bank holiday or storm escalation. Work orders at the state held when the plan for their day was published. ☐ |

The full specification for each — category definitions, the procedure a
colleague follows, and what the resulting count would and would not license —
is in the project's own file under `lib/cases/`. Ask before running one: the
definitions matter more than the arithmetic, and a category redefined halfway
through makes the whole extract unusable.

Two of these are worth running whether or not the pages ever publish them. The
intake count and the property gap check are each a week of one person's
attention and both would tell the client something they do not currently know.

## 3. A compliance gap you should close

The site shows "© Quiet Gears Ltd" and a London office address, but no company
registration number and no explicit registered-office statement.

A UK limited company must disclose, on its website, its registered company name,
its registered number, the part of the UK in which it is registered, and its
registered office address (Companies Act 2006, and the Companies (Trading
Disclosures) Regulations). The footer and the legal pages are the usual place.

Send the number and registered office and it takes five minutes to add. ☐
