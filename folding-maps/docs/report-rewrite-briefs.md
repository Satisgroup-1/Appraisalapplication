# Report rewrite briefs and evidence ledger

This document records the narrative decisions behind the dual-depth publication library. It is an editorial control document, not a source of client performance claims.

## Evidence classification

- **Regulatory or professional guidance:** an authoritative requirement or practice statement within its stated jurisdiction.
- **Independent research:** a study, survey or synthesis whose population, task and period constrain any inference.
- **Vendor-reported outcome:** a result published by a supplier or customer story. It remains scoped to that implementation and is not an industry forecast.
- **Engagement evidence:** observations produced by an identified Quiet Gears engagement. Current case status governs what can be claimed.
- **Management interpretation:** Quiet Gears analysis connecting evidence to an operating decision.
- **Illustrative design:** a proposed architecture, weighting, target or scenario that has not been measured in a client operation.

## News briefs

### AI integration gap

Reader question: Why does broad experimentation so often fail to become a dependable operating capability? Thesis: value depends on connecting a bounded workflow to governed records, ownership and evaluation, while model access alone leaves the coordination problem intact. The composite opening follows an enquiry copied across several tools and returns to the manager deciding which record can be trusted. The evidence hierarchy begins with UK Business Data Survey integration figures, then DSIT and OECD adoption research, with DORA used as an operating interpretation. Retain the two adoption and size exhibits. Consolidate repeated adoption claims. The reader should decide which workflow, record owner and acceptance measure to fund.

### Open-weight price war

Reader question: What should a smaller firm do when model prices and benchmark gaps fall quickly? Thesis: cheaper and more substitutable models improve buyer leverage only when the application has portable interfaces, representative evaluations and a complete cost model. The opening follows a procurement team comparing a low token price with the cost of operating the surrounding service. Stanford AI Index data supplies market context; NIST supplies lifecycle risk framing. Vendor prices remain time-sensitive inputs. The decision is whether to build portability and testing into the application before choosing a supplier.

### Automation before agents

Reader question: How much authority should a business grant an AI system? Thesis: authority should expand only after deterministic workflow controls and representative evaluation show that the system can handle the relevant states and failures. The composite opening follows an urgent service request that appears safe until an exception crosses a tool boundary. METR, OECD, HBS and DSIT evidence supports task heterogeneity and adoption maturity. Remove generic autonomy rhetoric. The management decision concerns tool access, approval gates, rollback and monitoring.

### Cold-chain collaboration

Reader question: How can continuous telemetry improve response without weakening food-safety accountability or OT security? Thesis: operational value arises when validated telemetry, current equipment context, versioned policy and corrective-action evidence form one reviewable exception case. The opening follows a temperature excursion whose interpretation changes after defrost and loading context is added. FSA and NCSC guidance are primary; NIST supports the governance model. The decision is whether the monitoring route can run in observation mode with controlled OT boundaries and measured alert quality.

### Small teams and AI advantage

Reader question: Can a lean specialist firm convert AI into an operating advantage? Thesis: leadership proximity and narrow workflows can shorten learning cycles, provided the firm concentrates ownership, data and evaluation on one material constraint. The composite opening follows a specialist team deciding whether to hire, defer work or redesign a recurring task. OECD and DSIT evidence constrains claims about workforce effects and readiness. Remove claims that imply an inherent size advantage. The decision is which bottleneck merits a governed pilot and how released capacity will be used.

### Measuring automation value

Reader question: When does released task time become business value? Thesis: a credible benefits case traces baseline activity through adoption, exceptions and redeployment to a defined service, cash, quality or risk outcome. The opening follows a finance review in which nominal hours saved cannot be found in capacity or cash. QJE and METR evidence show task-specific effects; DSIT and DORA frame organisational conversion. Keep the illustrative value bridge clearly labelled. The decision is which benefits ledger, baseline and owner will govern the release.

### Source-grounded legal work

Reader question: Under what conditions can a UK legal team rely on model-assisted research and drafting? Thesis: assistance becomes professionally useful when every proposition remains matter-scoped, traceable to an authoritative source, valid for the relevant date and jurisdiction and subject to accountable legal sign-off. A labelled composite scene follows a research proposition from client question to citation check. The Law Society, SRA, ICO and UK judicial guidance form the primary evidence base. Ironclad, Harvey, DLA Piper, Robin AI and stp.one are vendor-reported implementation examples only. The decision concerns approved sources, confidentiality boundaries, evaluation sets and sign-off.

### Hospitality guest recovery

Reader question: How can a hospitality group recover a disrupted stay when the relevant facts sit across several systems? Thesis: effective recovery requires a reconciled guest identity, live property state, explicit entitlement and controlled compensation authority before generated language can improve the interaction. A labelled composite scene follows a guest whose room is unavailable after arrival. ICO guidance anchors privacy; first-party hotel and travel customer stories illustrate specific reported implementations. Vendor metrics remain scoped to those programmes. The decision concerns identity matching, integration ownership, escalation authority and recovery measurement.

## Case-study briefs

### Marine operating record

One representative enquiry moves from an inbox into a proposed customer and project record. The central question asks what the first release must record so the team can identify the customer, current state, next event, owner and exception without losing the personal relationship. Discovery evidence is engagement evidence, while the priority scores and future acceptance criteria remain design decisions. The turning point is identity resolution: a customer, vessel, enquiry and project cannot be treated as one undifferentiated record. The design therefore uses stable identities, event history, derived state, explicit ownership and staged migration. Current status remains In progress. The next release decision depends on traceability, duplicate handling, status assembly time and safe rollback.

### Cold-chain exception

An illustrative temperature excursion passes from sensor to gateway, validation, classification, owner and corrective-action record. The central question asks whether the exception case is reliable enough to change staff action. The turning point occurs when the design separates telemetry validity from product severity. OT connectivity, policy versioning and missing-data behavior become release controls. All weights, response targets and expected benefits are illustrative. The pilot decision depends on an observation-mode comparison with existing controls.

### Property transaction

An illustrative transaction follows an enquiry, document set, negotiation, readiness check and archive. The central question asks whether the operating record can state what stage the transaction occupies and which evidence permits the next transition. Document provenance and dependencies change the original assumption that activity volume indicates progress. The design becomes a state machine with gate criteria, source-linked fields and exception ownership. All allocation values and expected benefits remain illustrative. The release decision is limited to one repeatable transaction type.

### Professional-services intake

An illustrative referral becomes a candidate matter, passes completeness and conflict gates, receives a source-grounded summary and reaches professional acceptance. The central question asks which work can be system-led without crossing confidentiality or professional-decision boundaries. The turning point separates deterministic eligibility controls from model-assisted preparation. The model may propose a summary from approved fields; it cannot alter a control result or accept the matter. Every target remains illustrative. The release decision depends on omission tests, confidentiality review and professional acceptance criteria.

### Field-service planning

An illustrative urgent job enters a day plan with skills, parts, travel, service windows and existing commitments. The central question asks whether a proposed schedule is feasible and useful enough for a dispatcher to adopt. The turning point separates hard constraints from weighted objectives and records confidence in every input. Dispatcher overrides become evaluation evidence, not failure noise. All optimisation scores and benefits remain illustrative. A shadow-mode comparison must precede any production dispatch authority.

## Consolidation decisions

Across the corpus, standalone analyst lenses, repeated scorecards and duplicated risk summaries are removed when their substance already appears in the narrative. Each variant keeps one thesis, one conclusion and one action agenda. References remain centralised by slug. Shared components control typography, citations, exhibit behavior and accessibility; they do not set section counts, heading patterns or argument order.

Simple variants retain the decision, strongest evidence, principal mechanism and material caveat. Advanced variants add data lineage, architecture, controls, economics, failure modes, evaluation and release criteria. The two modes are written independently so the Simple version does not read as a clipped technical report.
